## Conventions across SDKs

1. Shared auth model (`API_KEY`, `INGEST_BASE_URL`, tenant/project IDs).
2. Standard telemetry primitives: log, metric, span, flush/shutdown.
3. Queueing and retry behavior documented per runtime.
4. Consistent troubleshooting playbooks (auth, transport, volume, latency).
