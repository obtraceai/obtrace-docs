title: Why Obtrace
description: Practical reasons teams adopt Obtrace
---

Obtrace prioritizes fast implementation with predictable production behavior.

## Why teams adopt it

1. One instrumentation contract across browser, backend, and edge.
2. Less environment drift between dev, preview, and production.
3. Hardening guidance to reduce data loss and transport failures.
4. Ready integration patterns for common deployment platforms.

## Best fit scenarios

- You need a standard telemetry model across multiple services.
- You want faster diagnosis between frontend and backend events.
- You need controlled rollout with per-environment key policies.
