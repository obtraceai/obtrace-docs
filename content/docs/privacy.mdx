# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

## What We Collect

- **Telemetry data**: Traces, logs, and metrics sent by your applications via Obtrace SDKs and integrations
- **Account data**: Email, team name, and billing information
- **Platform metadata**: Deployment IDs, project IDs, and service names from connected platforms (Vercel, Render, Railway, etc.)

## What We Do Not Collect

- Application source code
- Database contents or query results
- End-user personal data (PII is scrubbed at the ingest layer)
- Passwords, tokens, or secrets (detected and redacted automatically)

## How We Use It

Telemetry data is used to provide observability features: dashboards, incident detection, root cause analysis, and auto-fix suggestions. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.

## Data Retention

Telemetry data is retained according to your plan (7, 30, or 90 days) and deleted automatically after the retention period.

## Data Location

Data is processed and stored in the EU (Hetzner, Germany).

## Your Rights

You can request data export or deletion at any time by contacting support@obtrace.ai.
